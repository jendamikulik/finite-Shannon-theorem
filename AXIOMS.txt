`atomMeasure` has total mass `atomQ` and cylinder mass `cylMass h`.
`residualMeasure` is the infinite product of the residual kernels, and
`fullMeasure` is a probability whose cylinder of `h` has mass `pathMass h`.
Every ordinary source has atomic mass at most `atomQ`, and `fullMeasure`
attains that value. A countable exact seed exists if and only if `atomQ = 1`.
`projective_entropy_identity` is `infiniteCost = sup_n finiteCost` for
antitone countable seeds, with entropy in nats. The infima may be infinite.
It is not the comparison with `L_∞`, and it does not identify `G_*`.
The limit `atomQ = lim F_∞` is not a theorem.
No `sorry` or `admit` is used.

Every countable seed admits a ranked rearrangement that preserves cylinder sums,
the seed measure, entropy (nats), and ExactTo. Ranked finiteCost and infiniteCost
therefore equal the corresponding infima over all countable seeds.
LInf ι := sup_n envelopeMean (trunc ι n []) (bits).
log 2 · LInf ≤ infiniteCost ≤ log 2 · (LInf + shannonOverhead).
A finite-entropy exact seed exists if and only if LInf < ∞.
The comparison uses the explicit unit conversion nats = log 2 · bits.
It does not identify G_*. The limit atomQ = lim F_∞ is not a theorem.
No sorry or admit is used.
