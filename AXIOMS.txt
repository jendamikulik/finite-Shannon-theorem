Seed ranking and envelope bridge.

Every countable seed admits a ranked rearrangement that preserves cylinder sums,
the seed measure, entropy (nats), and ExactTo. Ranked finiteCost and infiniteCost
therefore equal the corresponding infima over all countable seeds.

--- 11 October 2026, W1 universality and the limit Rényi variable ---

Notes. W1 between laws on ℝ is formalized in its CDF form ∫_ℝ |F_μ - F_ν|.
`Sqrt.IncFamily.L1_tendsto` is ∫ |G_n(x√n) - F_{a,b}(x)| dx → 0 for a > 0 (Flim is

F_∞ = FInf (tail-integral formula, value +∞ allowed, E Z at α = 1);

hypothesis, and `one_seed_all_orders_law` restates the main theorem with RZlaw.

--- 11 October 2026, countable-seed universality and the law of Z ---

Notes. `Kernel.universality_countable` is the W1 (CDF-L1) universality corollary for
countable seeds G n exact to horizon n with finite entropy and (E J_n - C_n)/√n → 0
(E J_n = `bits` = H(G n)/ln 2). `RenyiInf.lawZ` is an actual probability measure on
EReal: the Lebesgue–Stieltjes measure of the right-continuous F_∞ (`FInf_rightCont`)
plus mass 1 - atomQ at ⊤; `lawZ_tail` gives P(Z > t) = 1 - F_∞(t).
`moment_lt`, `moment_one`, `moment_gt` identify the Lebesgue integrals of
2^{(1-α)z} and z against lawZ with the tail-formula values; `RZmeas_eq` gives
R_α(Z) (from lawZ) = sup_n R_α(Z_n) for all α > 0. Mathlib (this version) has no
Wasserstein/optimal-transport definition, so W1 stays in its CDF form.
